## RQ5 — Security–utility Pareto (one-paragraph readout)

**Lead with the real cost, not the flat axis.** On the three SPECIFIED utility metrics (faithfulness, relevancy, query-success) the gap across all 6 cells is essentially zero (U3: C00000 0.891 vs C11111 0.887, Δ +0.004) — but that flatness is an **artifact**: all three are blind to L2 retrieval-redaction's actual failure mode, where the model returns *faithful but empty* non-answers (BEN-007/009/025 under the full stack score faithfulness 1.0 and count as query-success 'wins', yet context-precision 0.0). The dimension that exposes it, context-precision, drops ~0.10 (0.755→0.658) and latency rises ~1.9× (3727→6942 ms); both costs sit in the L2-bearing cells (C01000, C01100, C11111). On the honest utility axis U4 (adds context-precision) the full stack visibly trades 0.857→0.830. **On the security axis (judged-primary) the cells split into two near-tie clusters separated by a real >0.08 gap, and the split aligns EXACTLY with L2 presence:** a 'with-L2' secure cluster (C01000/C01100/C11111, judged ASR ≈0.17–0.19) and a '~baseline' cluster (C00000/C10000/C00100, ≈0.29–0.37). Adding L2 is the *single* move that buys security; on this single-turn-dominated scenario mix L1-only (C10000) and L3-only (C00100) are **redundant in aggregate** — same utility, security no better than baseline within noise (C10000, C00100); this bounds their *aggregate* value, not L3's per-family / multi-turn role (which is why L3 is retained in the recommended knee). **The noise-aware Pareto frontier runs C00000 → C01100 (knee) → C11111, and the knee / RQ5 optimum is C01100 (L2+L3)**: it captures the entire *measured* security benefit (judged ASR 0.192, tied with the full stack's 0.173, Δ within the 0.08 floor) at the lowest layer count (C01000 = L2 alone is co-located and even more minimal). **Recommendation — (a) high-risk orgs: C11111**, on a *defense-in-depth* basis (its ASR edge over the knee is within noise, not a measured win; justified by L1/L4/L5 covering attack surfaces this benign set doesn't exercise), accepting the ~0.10 context-precision and ~1.9× latency cost; **(b) utility-sensitive orgs: C01100** (or C01000), the knee — all the measured security at fewer layers, lower latency, no L1/L4 over-refusal risk on genuine benign traffic.
