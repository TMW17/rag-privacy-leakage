Knee = C01100 (L2+L3) — it captures essentially all of the full stack’s measured security (judged 0.192 vs 0.173; gap +0.019, within the 0.08 floor) at two layers. A naive Pareto wrongly picks the full stack on a within-noise edge; the full stack is a defense-in-depth choice, not a measured win over the knee.

Utility cost (Task 3.1, C00000 vs C11111): context-precision 0.755 → 0.658 (−0.097), latency ~1.9×; the three spec-metrics look flat because they miss L2’s faithful-but-empty answers (BEN-007/009/025).
