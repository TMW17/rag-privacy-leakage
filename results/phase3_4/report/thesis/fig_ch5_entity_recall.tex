  \caption[Per-entity-type Presidio detection recall]{Presidio detection recall for the principal
  entity types on the synthetic corpus (Task~0.1 measurement), each type's severity in parentheses.
  Every bar spans all instances of a type, split into the share Presidio \emph{detects} (teal, the recall)
  and the share that \emph{survives} the redaction layer (crimson); bars are sorted so the worst blind
  spots sit at the top. The high-severity categories Presidio never flags appear as full crimson bars at

  (\texttt{SURNAME\_TITLE}, $0.51$), while structured identifiers such as \texttt{NATIONAL\_ID} are caught
